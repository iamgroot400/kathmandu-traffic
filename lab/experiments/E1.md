# E1 - Calibrated fixed-time baseline

- **Status:** done
- **Owner:** human
- **Tests hypothesis:** none — this is the baseline every H is measured against, not a hypothesis test itself
- **Prediction (written BEFORE running):** (not recorded prospectively — baseline was calibrated iteratively against mean speed, not predicted)
- **Method:** 233-node/590-link arterial network, 30% of the 260k-trip gravity-model matrix, 128 fixed-time signals (90s cycle), within-day DUO routing, tmax=14400s.
- **Metric & baselines:** mean speed, delay ratio, completion %. Calibration target: observed Kathmandu peak-hour speed range.
- **Assumptions relied on:** A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A7, A10
- **Budget:** single run, ~2 min
- **Code & command:** `python scripts/run_baseline.py` (and the 15-seed version inside `scripts/compare.py`, config `fixed_time`)
- **Results:** `results/comparison.partial.csv` (config=fixed_time), n=15 seeds at tmax=14400: delay 2351±165s, mean speed 13.26 km/h (single-seed run at tmax=10800 showed 14.3 km/h / delay ratio 0.79 before the tmax fix).
- **Interpretation:** Usable baseline — not gridlocked, not free-flowing. 2/15 seeds still land below 99% completion even at tmax=14400, which the household of seeds (not just one) needs watching before quoting precise baseline numbers.
