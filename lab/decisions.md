## D6 - Count-tuning experiments, split fixed before any run (2026-11-18)
- **Goal (user):** tune the model until modelled entering volumes match the JICA 2011-12 counts, GEH < 5, at 6 of 6 junctions.

Held out: Balaju, Kalanki, Koteshwor. `scripts/tune_counts.py` never prints the held-out three unless `--reveal-test`, used once on the chosen configuration.

- **Round 1, one lever per public notebook (5 seeds each, next to the unchanged baseline):** 6a peak factor K 0.12/0.14/0.16; 6b outer zones load on main roads only (4/2/1 junctions); 6c side-street capacity 0.25/0.18/0.12 and 0.25 at 20 km/h; 6d loading junctions 8, intrazonal trips 50%/100% on the network, and both.

- **Smoke test (one local run, all levers at once, 2 h horizon):** gridlock (38% completed, inner roads 7 km/h) and the busiest tuning junctions got less traffic (New Baneshwor 19% of count). Suggests the busiest junctions are capacity-bound, not demand-starved; if round 1 confirms it, round 2 should test junction capacity.

## D7 - Round 1 result: busy junctions are capacity-bound; round 2 on capacity (2026-11-18)
- **Round 1 (6a-6d, 5 seeds each), best per lever on the 6 tuning junctions:** none passes more than 1 of 6 (Kalimati). Peak factor 0.12-0.16 jams the network (completion 82% -> 48%) and *lowers* the busy junctions (New Baneshwor 41% -> 24% of count). Outer zones on main roads at 1 junction: Kalimati passes (GEH 1.3), completion 96%, inner roads 17.3 km/h, busy four unchanged. Side-street capacity and loading points: no gain at the busy four.
- **Diagnosis (one baseline dump, 6a seed 1):** every approach to the busy junctions is queued at 3-25x free-flow travel time, each lane passing about 500-1,000 PCU/h; counts need roughly double. Several approaches have 1-2 lanes in OpenStreetMap.
- **Round 2 (7a-7d):** `run_baseline.py` gained `--reaction-time` (UXsim per-lane saturation headway; default 1 s unchanged) and `--min-main-lanes` (default 0, off). 7a lanes >=2 / >=3 (+2 with round-1 entry roads); 7b reaction time 0.8 / 0.67 / 0.5 s; 7c cycle 60 / 120 / 150 s; 7d entry roads + reaction 0.67 or 0.5 + 2 lanes.

- **Round 2 (7a-7d), best:** 7d `gw1_rt0.5_lanes2` passes 2 of 6 (Chabahil 1.01, Old Baneshwor 0.99), tuning-junction share 0.59 -> 0.90, mean GEH 43 -> 16, but Ring Road 33.7 / inner 33.5 km/h vs survey 24.0 / 15.3. 7b reaction 0.67 s passes Chabahil and Gausala (27.6 / 20.3 km/h); 0.8 s keeps speeds near the survey (23.3 / 18.0) at share 0.70. 7a 3 lanes: share 0.77, Chabahil passes, 22.2 / 18.0 km/h. 7c signal cycle: little effect.
