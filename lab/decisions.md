## D9 - Round 3 result and round 4: junction measure, mid-block loading, OD fitting (2026-11-18)
- **Round 3 (8a-8d):** total volume solved but not its pattern. 8d k0.14 carries 99% of the tuning counts and passes 0/6 (Kalimati 1.58, New Baneshwor 0.78). Most realistic: 8c 3 lanes + k0.13 (87%, 19.5 / 16.9 km/h, 0/6) and 8b rt0.67 + k0.14 (81%, 18.0 / 16.1 km/h, 2/6: Chabahil, Gausala). In all 12 settings Kalimati (west) is 1.14-1.80 and the Baneshwor/Thapathali junctions 0.4-0.96: a spatial error no global lever fixes.
- **User chose (round 4):** fix the junction measure, re-place zone demand, fit OD flows to counts.
- **Junction measure:** each counted junction = traffic entering all nodes within **100 m** of its pinned node from outside that group (`checks/geh_jica2012.py --radius`; radius fixed before rescoring). Default 0 keeps the published validation unchanged. On the baseline: Thapathali 0.51 -> 0.59, Chabahil 0.69 -> 0.77, Kalimati 1.14 -> 1.22.
- **Demand placement flaw found:** zones loaded trips at their highest-degree nodes, i.e. the big junctions: 1,860 PCU/h at each Baneshwor node, 690 at Thapathali, 1,165 Balaju, 1,350 Koteshwor. Such trips bypass the approaches (not counted) and the signal. `build_od_jica.py --midblock`: load at nodes with <= 2 neighbours, >= 50 m from any junction and >= 100 m from a counted one; checked 0 PCU/h then loads at counted nodes; base output unchanged.
- **OD fitting (`scripts/fit_od_counts.py`):** path-based multiplicative OD estimation on the 6 tuning junctions only: r = count / model (100 m measure), shortest paths on simulated travel times, factor = product of r^0.5 over tuning junctions entered, clipped to [1/1.5, 1.5] per iteration, whole platoons; 3 seeds per iteration, up to 6 iterations. One offline update step checked on existing runs (769 pairs up, 6 down, 58,525 -> 63,020 PCU/h).
- **Notebooks:** 9a mid-block loading x best capacity settings (scored at radius 0 and 100); 9b OD fit from mid-block + rt 0.67 + k 0.14; 9c OD fit from mid-block + 3 lanes + k 0.13.
