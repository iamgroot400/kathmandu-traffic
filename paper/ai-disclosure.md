- **Implementation**: simulation code (network import, demand generation, each intervention's implementation, statistical comparison scripts) was written by the assistant to the authors' specifications and run under the authors' direction; the authors reviewed each result before proceeding.
- **Mechanical drafting**: this manuscript's prose was drafted by the assistant from the project's recorded experimental results and the authors' own research record, at the authors' direction, following a claim ledger the authors reviewed. No results, numbers, or citations were invented by either the authors or the assistant; the authors are responsible for every claim this paper makes.
- **Verification support**: literature searches and citation checks were run by the assistant and reviewed by the authors; a data-entry error the assistant introduced (a fabricated DOI) was caught during the authors' review process and corrected.
- **Error correction**: a statistical error in an intermediate working conclusion (the originally assumed "super-additive" interaction between the two levers) was identified and corrected during the authors' own review of the draft, before submission — see Results, Section 3.
